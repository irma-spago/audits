FlyRank / Helimeds on-page SEO audit

Meta, H1 and images, page by page

Current and recommended values for every page and blog article on helimeds.com: meta title, meta description, H1, and each image’s file name, alt text and format. Numbers next to titles and descriptions are character counts.

5 pagesHomepage & core pages10 pagesTreatment pages6 pagesLegal & policy pages11 pagesAuthor pages2 pagesPromo pages127 articlesBlog articles
What this audit covers
  • Meta titles
  • Meta descriptions
  • H1 headings
  • Image file names
  • Image ALT text
  • Image file formats

Target lengths: titles 50–60 characters, descriptions 140–155. Counts outside a healthy range are marked in amber. Every recommended description is unique.

Biggest fixes
  • 15 pages have no H1, including all 10 treatment pages, where the product name is a styled <div>. Changing that element to <h1> in the shared product template fixes all 10.
  • Several product images are uploaded as raw PNGs of up to 2 MB (Scream Cream, MIC+B12, Tadalafil, Sildenafil, NAD+). Serve them through next/image as WebP, like the rest of the site.
  • 53 of 127 article titles run past 60 characters, and 60 descriptions run past 160, so search results cut them off.
  • Two articles have copied metadata: “How Much Sildenafil Is Too Much?” uses the BlueChew article’s H1, and the NAD injections article uses the sildenafil article’s description.
  • Most page-specific images have empty, filename-style or “Image” alt text, and 21 article images just repeat the H1. Five Tadalafil product images show injection vials, not tablets.
