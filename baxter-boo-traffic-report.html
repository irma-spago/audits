06 / Recovery

What drives the recovery

The blog has already made up about two-thirds of the clicks lost on category and product pages. Full recovery depends on those pages.

  • Implement the audit on category and product pages. These sections lost about 16,000 clicks year over year and are the main reason total traffic is down.
  • Fix the 840 pages that lost all visibility. Redirect removed categories and discontinued products to the closest live page.
  • Keep publishing blog content. It has grown every month since Jun 19 and leads the site in AI features.
  • Review progress monthly using the same year-over-year windows.
