Conclusion

What the data says about competition between these posts.

  • The posts are not competing today. Each post has its own main searches. #1 gets "deodorant without baking soda" and its natural and men's variants. #2 gets comparisons like "magnesium vs baking soda deodorant". #3 gets packaging searches. #5 gets "best baking-soda-free deodorant" questions. #6 gets sensitive skin and rash searches. #7 gets "baking soda for underarms". The searches they share have 1 to 60 impressions each and no clicks.
  • #2 and #5 share the most searches. Five of #5's top 10 searches also show for #2, all comparison and "best" questions. #2 ranks higher on the two biggest ones (position 2.4 vs 5.4, and 5.9 vs 10.2). Linking #5 to #2 for comparison questions will help Google treat #2 as the comparison page.
  • Watch the sensitive-skin posts (#4, #6 and #7). All three cover baking-soda-free deodorant for sensitive skin. #6 and #7 both show for "baking soda free deodorant for rash prone armpits" (#6: 34 impressions at position 7.2, #7: 8 at 9.2). #4 isn't indexed yet. If competition appears, it will most likely appear here first.
  • #4 and #8 aren't indexed yet. URL Inspection confirms Google hasn't indexed either post, so neither has Search data. We're waiting for Google to index them. Once it does, we'll check #4 against #6 and #7, and #8 against #1, since #8 covers the same topic: natural deodorant without baking soda.
