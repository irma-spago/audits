Where the new clicks come from

  • The blog drives the growth. Blog posts brought 159 clicks in the last 28 days, up from 33 in the 28 days before optimization.
  • Breath Mints: An Oral History grew from 10 to 61 clicks, and Bad Breath Pills went from 0 to 33.
  • New non-brand searches bring clicks, such as "best breath mints", "ancient egypt breath mints" and "natural breath freshener".
