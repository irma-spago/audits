Clicks will follow impressions

  • Impressions come first. Google is testing the new pages for many queries (753 so far). Average position is 24.5, which is mostly page 2 and 3 of results, where few people click.
  • Clicks start as pages reach page 1. Six blog pages already average a top-10 position: rosuvastatin and magnesium, folinic acid 25 mg, cyanocobalamin 1000 mcg, folate DFE, L-serine vs phosphatidylserine and homocysteine urine test. These are the first likely sources of steady clicks.
  • Most clicks today are brand searches. 18 of 37 clicks went to the homepage, mostly from "otherway health" and "otherway".
Too early for firm conclusions. One month is a short window for SEO, and rankings for new content take time to settle. The numbers so far show the right direction: Google is indexing the content and showing it for more searches every week.

05 / Products

Product snippet warnings

FlyRank can fix

All 4 product pages are valid. The warnings are missing optional fields. Adding them lets Google show more product details in search, such as stars, returns and shipping.

Missing fieldWhat it meansItems
aggregateRatingThe average star rating and review count. Shows stars under the product in search.2
reviewIndividual customer reviews in the product's structured data.2
hasMerchantReturnPolicyThe return policy, such as the return window and who pays return shipping.4
shippingDetailsShipping cost and delivery time.4
descriptionA short product description in the structured data.3

Rating and review warnings affect Calm & Collected Duo (methylation-bundle) and Methyl Balance. Return, shipping and description warnings come from the Merchant listings report.

How FlyRank fixes it

  • Add return policy and shipping details to the product structured data, using the store's existing shipping and refund policies.
  • Add product descriptions to the 3 products missing them.
  • Connect real reviews to the product markup once products have customer reviews. Google only allows ratings from real customers.
  • Validate the fix in Search Console so Google rechecks the pages.
