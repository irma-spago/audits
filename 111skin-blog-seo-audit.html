111Journal Blog & Article Audit

We did a technical and on-page SEO audit of the /blogs/111journal blog index page and its articles. The index shows 51 article cards, but 5 of them repeat, so we audited 46 articles. We also flagged UX and UI issues we found along the way.

Summary

31 RULES

Every page is crawlable, indexable and correctly canonicalised. The problems are in structure, trust signals and links. The index has no working pagination and its cards have no excerpts, dates or authors. The articles have incomplete or missing Article schema, "111SKIN Store" as the author, no breadcrumbs, and broken CTAs and product links.

Biggest issues: ?page=2 of the blog index is a duplicate of page 1. 12 posts have a CTA that links to undefined, and 17 posts link to removed products that now redirect to the homepage.

Priority fixes

IN ORDER

Ordered by SEO impact, with the most important fix first.

Blog index

  1. HighRule 14
    Fix pagination. Either make ?page=N serve the next set of posts, with crawlable <a href> links between pages, or stop page 2 from being served as a duplicate.
  2. HighRule 12
    Complete the article cards. Add an excerpt, a visible publish date and the author to every card, show full titles in the HTML, and remove the 5 duplicate cards.
  3. HighRules 5, 6
    Add structured data. Add Blog schema listing the posts and a BreadcrumbList (Home › Journal), and fill or remove the empty sameAs entries.
  4. MediumRules 7, 8, 9
    Fix the content and headings. Add 50+ words of intro copy under the H1, rename the H1 to "The 111Journal", and change the announcement bar, cart and currency headings to non-heading elements.
  5. MediumRule 10Tabs
    Fix navigation and search. Merge the two "Wellness" tags, and add a "View all" link under each tab that goes to its blog page. Add a new search bar to the blog page that searches journal articles.
  6. MediumRules 11, 13
    Images and featured post. Add alt text to the 35 card images that lack it, rename the generic image files, and add a featured post above the grid.
  7. LowRules 2, 15
    Quick wins. Lengthen the meta description to 140–160 characters, switch og:image to https://, and fill in twitter:image.

Article pages

  1. HighRule 12UX
    Fix the broken links. Point the "Discover our bestsellers" button to a real collection on posts 1–7, 16 and 39, and remove the "undefined" links on posts 28, 29 and 41. Replace the 13 removed-product links, the 2 deleted-article links and the "-old" URLs with current pages.
  2. HighRules 5, 8, 13
    Fix the article schema and authors. Add BlogPosting schema to the 8 newer-layout posts. Add dateModified and an author with name and url to every post. Replace "111SKIN Store" with the real author, linked to an author page, and make the visible date match datePublished.
  3. HighRules 6, 7
    Add breadcrumbs and fix the H1s. Add a Home › Journal › Post trail with BreadcrumbList to the article template. Demote body H1s to H2 in the 10 posts that have several, and add a one-sentence summary under each title.
  4. MediumRules 8, 10UX
    Combine the two layouts. Move the 38 older posts to one article layout that shows the date under the title, uses H2 and H3 for section headings with no empty or skipped levels, and gives featured images a width and height.
  5. MediumRules 1, 2
    Rewrite titles and descriptions. Bring the 30 titles into 50–60 characters and the 16 descriptions into 140–160 characters, starting with posts 18, 28 and 13.
  6. MediumRules 11, 14, 15
    Fill content gaps. Add related posts to the 12 posts that have none, and a table of contents to the 6 long posts without one (also fix the 4 broken anchors). Expand or merge the 14 posts under 600 words, starting with the large-pores series.
  7. LowImagesUX
    Clean up images and layout. Add alt text and dimensions to inline images, rename the generic image files, fix the duplicate prev/next links, and tighten the alternating layout so text isn't left beside large empty space.
