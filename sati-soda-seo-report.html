05 / Takeaway

Most clicks now come from FlyRank content.

Since launch, FlyRank articles brought in 1,192 of 2,187 clicks and more than 9 in 10 AI impressions, and non-branded search grew from about half of clicks to 77.1%. CTR fell from 4.12% to 0.67% as the site began showing up for far more questions, so it's worth watching as the new pages climb.
