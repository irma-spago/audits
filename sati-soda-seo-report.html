08 / Takeaway

Daily clicks are up 51.8%, led by FlyRank content.

The average day now brings 31.9 clicks and 4,072 impressions, up from 21.0 and 244. FlyRank articles bring in 55.8% of search clicks and 91.6% of AI impressions. CTR fell from 8.61% to 0.78% as visibility grew much faster than clicks, and branded clicks per day dipped, so both are worth watching.
